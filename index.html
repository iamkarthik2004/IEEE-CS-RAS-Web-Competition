About Us

💻 Computer Society (CS)

The Computer Society is the premier professional organization for computer scientists and engineers. We provide resources, publications, and opportunities to advance computing.

🤖 Robotics & Automation Society (RAS)

The Robotics and Automation Society focuses on advancing innovation, education, and fundamental and applied research in robotics and automation.

🎯 Our Mission

To be the leading provider of technical information, community services, and personalized services to computing professionals worldwide.

👁️ Our Vision

To be the community of choice for aspiring and practicing professionals in all areas of computing and allied fields.
